# Order-Logistics Recommendations

_Generated by `src/inventory.py` from DataCo order history (101 products). Assumptions: 2-week supplier lead time, $50/order, 25%/yr holding cost._

## 1. Fix the shipping promises (biggest, cheapest win)
- **First Class**: promised 1 d, actual 2.0 d, **100% late** (15% of order lines).
- **Second Class**: promised 2 d, actual 4.0 d, **80% late** (20% of order lines).
- **Standard Class**: promised 4 d, actual 4.0 d, **40% late** (60% of order lines).
- **Same Day**: promised 0 d, actual 0.5 d, **48% late** (5% of order lines).
- Second Class arrives in 4.0 days, the same as Standard (4.0). Customers pay a premium for zero speed. **Action:** re-quote First Class as 2-day and Second Class as 4-day (or retire Second Class), and audit the carrier SLA behind First Class: it misses its 1-day promise on every order.
- Late rate is ~flat across all five markets, so this is a promise-setting problem, not a regional carrier problem. Fixing the quoted days alone removes most "late" deliveries without moving a single package faster.

## 4. What the forecast is worth
- Sizing safety stock on Prophet's forecast error instead of the seasonal-naive forecast's error frees **$5,364** of inventory across the 9 forecasted products at the same service level.
- Prophet lowers safety stock on 7 of 9 products; where naive wins, keep the naive forecast for that SKU. Pick the model per product, not one model for the whole catalog.

## 5. Watch list
- Demand anomalies (`outputs/anomalies.csv`): treat **high/medium** severity weeks as signal; low-severity flags are within what a 95% band produces by chance.
